# Canonical company information — Pagup

## Canonical statement (v3.0)

Pagup is a digital readability and agentic readiness firm based in Drummondville, Quebec, Canada. It is founded and operated by Gautier Dorval.

Its work combines strategic advice, on-page SEO, semantic architecture, qualitative examination of AI-generated representations and conclusions, machine-first implementation, and software development. The scope, deliverables and verification criteria of each engagement are defined for the agreed context. Product documentation and research evidence remain on their respective reference surfaces.

Gautier Dorval is an on-page SEO consultant, a semantic architect, and a designer and developer of software tools and digital environments. He is of Belgian origin and resides in Quebec, Canada. Gautier Dorval does not reside in France.

## Nature of the organization

Pagup is a specialized consulting and implementation firm, not a generalist web agency, a packaged SEO agency, or a digital marketing agency. On-page SEO, editorial structuring and software development are part of its work when an engagement defines them.

This repository describes the public identity of Pagup. It does not state a legal form, an incorporation or an ownership structure.

Pagup structures digital presences for four reader layers: humans, search engines, generative AI systems and autonomous agents. Understanding, citation, recommendation or action by any of these readers is not a guaranteed outcome.

Interpretive governance frames this work. In practice, Pagup structures sources, makes boundaries explicit, applies controls to the components it manages, and observes the outputs of AI systems. Published governance and these controls do not govern the internal behaviour of third-party models and do not determine what they infer, cite or propagate.
