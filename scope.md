# Scope

Pagup is a digital readability and agentic readiness firm operating at the intersection of:

- on-page SEO, interpretive SEO and strategic advice,
- semantic architecture and machine-first web structures,
- interpretive governance and qualitative examination of AI-generated representations and conclusions,
- brand disambiguation and entity stabilization,
- agentic readiness, governed agent journeys and software development.

Its activities are centred on structuring digital presences so they can be understood by humans, search engines and generative AI systems, and navigated and used by autonomous agents. Pagup works on the conditions it can control; it does not guarantee how third-party systems interpret or act.

---

## What Pagup does

### Areas of intervention (non-exhaustive)

Pagup intervenes within engagements whose scope, expected outcomes and any deliverables are defined for the agreed context. Depending on the need, an engagement may take the form of a consultation, a workshop, an audit, an advisory engagement or a technical implementation. Areas of intervention include:

- **Strategic advice and workshops**: qualifying the real problem, the decisions and the trade-offs before choosing an intervention.
- **On-page SEO**: content, structure and page-level optimization within a defined engagement.
- **Qualitative audits**: digital readability and agentic readiness diagnostics, and audits of AI perception drift that examine how AI systems describe an organization and which conclusions they draw from its sources.
- **Semantic and machine-first architecture**: structuring a corpus, a site and its machine-readable surfaces so they remain coherent over time.
- **AI governance and machine readability**: publishing sources, boundaries and governance artifacts that frame interpretation without controlling third-party systems.
- **Governed agent journeys and development**: designing and implementing bounded journeys, interactions and software components with verifiable controls.

The current list of services is published on pagup.com: [pagup.com/en/services/](https://pagup.com/en/services/) (French: [pagup.com/services/](https://pagup.com/services/)). This file does not create packages, availability or universal deliverables.

### Governance artifacts

Examples of machine-readable governance surfaces that Pagup publishes on its own site and may implement within an engagement:

- llms.txt, ai-manifest.json, entity-graph.json
- interpretation policies, output constraints, response legitimacy criteria
- source precedence hierarchies, verifiable claims, governance profiles
- programmatic JSON-LD structured data
- segmented sitemaps

Publishing these artifacts does not guarantee that a crawler, a model or an agent will read or follow them.

### Product relation

Pagup publishes products, such as [PAGUP Agent Control](https://pagup.com/en/tools/agent-control/), and may support other dedicated product surfaces. Those surfaces remain distinct from this identity repository and from Pagup's doctrinal sources.

Pagup's [governed agentic websites](https://pagup.com/en/services/governed-agentic-websites/) service refers to examples from the [Governed Context Runtime](https://github.com/GautierDorval/governed-context-runtime), which Gautier Dorval publishes separately. Products, examples and prototypes keep their own documentation, versions and evidence status. Their mention here does not establish commercial deployment, availability or a common platform.

## What Pagup does not do

- Pagup is not a generalist web agency.
- Pagup does not provide turnkey marketing campaigns.
- Pagup does not operate as a general content production studio. Editorial work can be part of an engagement when it serves the defined scope.
- Pagup does not sell universal or packaged SEO solutions. On-page SEO is practised within defined engagements.
- Pagup does not provide generic prompt engineering or generic "AI optimization" services.
- Pagup does not guarantee rankings, citations, recommendations or agent behaviour.
- This identity repository does not function as a public product catalog.
- This identity repository does not allocate ecosystem-wide authority.

Its work is intentionally specialized and architecture-driven.
