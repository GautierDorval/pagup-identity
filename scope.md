# Scope

Pagup is a specialized cabinet operating at the intersection of:

- interpretive governance and AI readability,
- semantic architecture and machine-first web structures,
- brand disambiguation and entity stabilization,
- interpretive SEO and digital readability.

Its activities are centered on structuring digital presences so they are correctly interpreted by humans, search engines, generative AI systems, and autonomous agents.

---

## What Pagup does

### Six service lines

1. **Strategic digital readability diagnostic** — Qualifying the real problem before choosing an intervention.
2. **Machine-first / AI-first website rebuild** — Reconstructing a site as a readable infrastructure for all reading layers.
3. **AI governance and machine readability** — Deploying governance artifacts that stabilize what AI systems understand.
4. **Brand disambiguation and stabilization** — Clarifying what an organization is, what it does, and what it is not.
5. **Semantic content architecture** — Structuring a corpus so it becomes a durable asset, not accumulated noise.
6. **Strategic advisory and transformation** — Ongoing governance to maintain coherence over time and prevent interpretive debt.

### Governance artifacts deployed

Pagup publishes and maintains machine-first governance surfaces including:

- llms.txt, ai-manifest.json, entity-graph.json
- Interpretation policies, output constraints, response legitimacy criteria
- Source precedence hierarchies, verifiable claims, governance profiles
- JSON-LD programmatic structured data across all pages
- Segmented sitemaps (11 files, FR + EN)

### Published tooling

17 WordPress plugins with 32,000+ active installations, including:

- [Better Robots.txt](https://better-robots.com/) — Advanced robots.txt control
- [BIALTY](https://bialty.com/) — AI-powered bulk image alt text generation
- [Auto Links for SEO](https://autolinksforseo.com/) — Automatic internal linking
- [WP Google Street View](https://wpstreetview.com/) — Google Street View integration

## What Pagup does not do

- Pagup is not a generalist web agency.
- Pagup does not provide turnkey marketing campaigns.
- Pagup does not operate as a content production studio.
- Pagup does not sell universal or packaged SEO solutions.
- Pagup does not provide prompt engineering or "AI optimization" services.

Its work is intentionally specialized and architecture-driven.
