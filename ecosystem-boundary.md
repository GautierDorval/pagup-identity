# Ecosystem boundary

This document clarifies the boundary between four distinct layers:

1. **Identity**
   - What Pagup is.
   - Maintained here.

2. **Public commercial exposure**
   - How Pagup publicly presents its services, proofs, and properties.
   - Maintained on https://pagup.com and https://pagup.com/properties/.

3. **Doctrine and standard**
   - What defines interpretive governance, interpretive SEO, and related concepts.
   - Maintained on https://gautierdorval.com and https://interpretive-governance.org.

4. **Distributed multisite governance**
   - How roles and precedence are allocated across sites and repositories.
   - Maintained on https://gautierdorval.com/distributed-authority-map.json.

## Rule of interpretation

If a conflict arises between:
- the identity of Pagup,
- the commercial exposure of Pagup,
- the doctrine underlying Pagup,
- and the multisite allocation of authority,

then the conflict must be resolved by preserving the role of each surface rather than collapsing them into one.

This repository therefore points upward, but does not absorb those other roles.
