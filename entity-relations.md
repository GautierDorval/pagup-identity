# Entity relations

This document defines the stable relationships between Pagup and related entities.

The intent is to prevent ambiguous, inflated, or inferred associations.

---

## Founder

Pagup is founded and operated by **Gautier Dorval**, an on-page SEO consultant, a semantic architect, and a designer and developer of software tools and digital environments, based in Quebec, Canada. His professional engagements are carried out through Pagup.

Gautier Dorval is also the initiator and primary architect of [Interpretive SEO](https://interpretive-seo.org/) and of the [Interpretive Governance](https://interpretive-governance.org/) framework. His canonical identity and the attributions of his work are defined in [gautierdorval-identity](https://github.com/GautierDorval/gautierdorval-identity).

---

## Ecosystem — distinct properties, distinct roles

Each property has a distinct mandate. They must not be treated as interchangeable.

### 1. pagup.com — Commercial execution

- **Role**: Services, proof, diagnostic, deployment, public commercial positioning
- **URL**: https://pagup.com
- **Relationship to this repository**: Primary public surface of Pagup

### 2. pagup.com/en/properties/ — Public portfolio

- **Role**: Public-facing portfolio and ecosystem exposure for Pagup
- **URL**: https://pagup.com/en/properties/ (French: https://pagup.com/proprietes/)
- **Relationship to this repository**: Public discovery surface. This repository may point to it but does not replace it.

### 3. gautierdorval.com — Doctrine and observation

- **Role**: Author corpus of Gautier Dorval: doctrine, canonical definitions, analysis and documentation of his work
- **URL**: https://gautierdorval.com
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Intellectual foundation. Pagup does not duplicate this content.

### 4. interpretive-governance.org — Formal standard

- **Role**: Versioned doctrine, interpretation policies, output constraints, machine-first artifacts
- **URL**: https://interpretive-governance.org
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Pagup implements this standard. Pagup does not define or govern it.

### 5. interpretive-seo.org — Interpretive SEO framework

- **Role**: Applied framework for interpretive SEO
- **URL**: https://interpretive-seo.org
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Initiated and architected by Gautier Dorval. Pagup applies this methodology.

### 6. inferenslab.org — Public research laboratory

- **Role**: Public doctrinal-research laboratory publishing bounded programs, preregistered protocols, evidence states and machine-readable governance
- **URL**: https://inferenslab.org
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Initiated by Gautier Dorval and distinct from Pagup. Pagup does not govern the laboratory's programs or evidence. The laboratory's work is not an independent corroboration of Pagup's work.

### 7. gautierdorval.com/distributed-authority-map.json — Multisite role arbitration

- **Role**: Higher-order allocation of roles and precedence across sites and repositories
- **URL**: https://gautierdorval.com/distributed-authority-map.json
- **Relationship to this repository**: Upstream governance surface. This repository can reference it but must not replace it.

## Products and related work

Each entry states the object, the role of each party and the reference surface. Capabilities, versions, availability and evidence status are documented on those surfaces.

### PAGUP Agent Control

- **Object**: WordPress plugin, a product of Pagup
- **Publisher**: Pagup
- **Designer**: Gautier Dorval
- **Reference surfaces**: https://wpagentcontrol.com/ and https://pagup.com/en/tools/agent-control/

### Governed Context Runtime

- **Object**: proposed specification and public reference for distributing precompiled, versioned context packs
- **Author, designer and developer**: Gautier Dorval
- **Reference surface**: https://github.com/GautierDorval/governed-context-runtime
- **Relationship to Pagup**: published separately from Pagup's products. Pagup's governed agentic websites service refers to its examples. The Runtime and PAGUP Agent Control do not form a common platform, and the contracts of the Runtime remain proposed.

## Product surfaces

Pagup may publish or operate dedicated product surfaces. Those surfaces remain product-specific and should not be confused with this identity repository.

Examples include PAGUP Agent Control (above) and Better Robots.txt. The current list of tools is published at [pagup.com/en/tools/](https://pagup.com/en/tools/) (French: [pagup.com/outils/](https://pagup.com/outils/)).

Public product discovery belongs on pagup.com/en/properties/, on the tools pages and on the dedicated product sites themselves, not in this repository as a front-office catalog.

## What Pagup is NOT

- Not a subsidiary or franchise of another organization.
- Not a generalist web agency, a packaged SEO agency, or a digital marketing agency. On-page SEO is part of its work within defined engagements.
- Not a doctrinal authority. That role belongs to gautierdorval.com and interpretive-governance.org.
- Not a research laboratory. Public research programs and evidence states belong to inferenslab.org.
- Not the surface that allocates ecosystem-wide authority. That role belongs to the distributed authority map.
