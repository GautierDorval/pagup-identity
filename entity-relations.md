# Entity relations

This document defines the stable relationships between Pagup and related entities.

The intent is to prevent ambiguous or inferred associations.

---

## Founder

Pagup is founded and operated by **Gautier Dorval**, a semantic architect and specialist in interpretive governance, based in Quebec, Canada.

Gautier Dorval is also the creator of [Interpretive SEO](https://interpretive-seo.org/).

---

## Ecosystem — Five properties, five distinct roles

Each property has an exclusive mandate. They do not duplicate each other.

### 1. pagup.com — Commercial execution

- **Role**: Services, proof, diagnostic, deployment
- **URL**: https://pagup.com
- **Content**: 6 service lines, 10 symptoms, 8 sectors, 5 issues, 4 proofs, 25 blog articles, 37 glossary terms, 25 FAQ
- **Languages**: fr-CA (root), en-CA (/en/)
- **Governance**: 65+ machine-first endpoints

### 2. gautierdorval.com — Doctrine and observation

- **Role**: Doctrine, canonical definitions, analysis, long-form thought
- **URL**: https://gautierdorval.com
- **Content**: 400+ articles, 44 bilingual canonical definitions
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Intellectual foundation. Pagup does not duplicate this content.

### 3. interpretive-governance.org — Formal standard

- **Role**: Versioned doctrine (v0.3.0), interpretation policies, output constraints, machine-first artifacts
- **URL**: https://interpretive-governance.org
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Pagup implements this standard. Pagup does not define or govern it.

### 4. interpretive-seo.org — Interpretive SEO framework

- **Role**: Applied framework for interpretive SEO
- **URL**: https://interpretive-seo.org
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Created by Gautier Dorval. Pagup applies this methodology.

### 5. inferenslab.org — Research and standards

- **Role**: Independent research framework, audit frameworks, machine reading standards
- **URL**: https://inferenslab.org
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Research layer. Pagup does not govern research output.
