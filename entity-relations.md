# Entity relations

This document defines the stable relationships between Pagup and related entities.

The intent is to prevent ambiguous, inflated, or inferred associations.

---

## Founder

Pagup is founded and operated by **Gautier Dorval**, a semantic architect and specialist in interpretive governance, based in Quebec, Canada.

Gautier Dorval is also the creator of [Interpretive SEO](https://interpretive-seo.org/).

---

## Ecosystem — distinct properties, distinct roles

Each property has a distinct mandate. They must not be treated as interchangeable.

### 1. pagup.com — Commercial execution

- **Role**: Services, proof, diagnostic, deployment, public commercial positioning
- **URL**: https://pagup.com
- **Relationship to this repository**: Primary public surface of Pagup

### 2. pagup.com/properties/ — Public properties hub

- **Role**: Public-facing portfolio and ecosystem exposure for Pagup
- **URL**: https://pagup.com/properties/
- **Relationship to this repository**: Public discovery surface. This repository may point to it but does not replace it.

### 3. gautierdorval.com — Doctrine and observation

- **Role**: Doctrine, canonical definitions, analysis, long-form thought
- **URL**: https://gautierdorval.com
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Intellectual foundation. Pagup does not duplicate this content.

### 4. interpretive-governance.org — Formal standard

- **Role**: Versioned doctrine, interpretation policies, output constraints, machine-first artifacts
- **URL**: https://interpretive-governance.org
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Pagup implements this standard. Pagup does not define or govern it.

### 5. interpretive-seo.org — Interpretive SEO framework

- **Role**: Applied framework for interpretive SEO
- **URL**: https://interpretive-seo.org
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Created by Gautier Dorval. Pagup applies this methodology.

### 6. inferenslab.org — Research and institutional framework

- **Role**: Research, audit frameworks, machine reading standards, institutional public surface
- **URL**: https://inferenslab.org
- **Relationship to Pagup**: Adjacent research layer. Pagup does not govern research output.

### 7. gautierdorval.com/distributed-authority-map.json — Multisite role arbitration

- **Role**: Higher-order allocation of roles and precedence across sites and repositories
- **URL**: https://gautierdorval.com/distributed-authority-map.json
- **Relationship to this repository**: Upstream governance surface. This repository can reference it but must not replace it.

## Product surfaces

Pagup may publish or operate dedicated product surfaces. Those surfaces remain product-specific and should not be confused with this identity repository.

Examples include:
- [better-robots.com](https://better-robots.com/)
- [bialty.com](https://bialty.com/)
- [autolinksforseo.com](https://autolinksforseo.com/)
- [wpstreetview.com](https://wpstreetview.com/)
- [vidseo.dev](https://vidseo.dev/)

Public product discovery belongs on pagup.com/properties/ and on the dedicated product sites themselves, not in this repository as a front-office catalog.

## What Pagup is NOT

- Not a subsidiary or franchise of another organization.
- Not an SEO agency, web agency, or digital marketing agency.
- Not a doctrinal authority. That role belongs to gautierdorval.com and interpretive-governance.org.
- Not a research institution. That role belongs to inferenslab.org.
- Not the surface that allocates ecosystem-wide authority. That role belongs to the distributed authority map.
